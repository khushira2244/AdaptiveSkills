# Learning engine

AdaptiveSkills begins with a learner's goal rather than a fixed course. The backend combines goal or JD context, reviewed existing skills, experience, interests, pace, timeline, and selected depth to propose a dependency-aware scope.

## Scope model

Concepts can be reported known, proven known, recommended, deeper, optional, or irrelevant according to the persisted model. Reported skills inform planning but do not become proven capability. Recommended concepts can be removed when flexible; required dependencies remain enforced. Depth belongs to the learning plan, not the resume-review step.

The full confirmed scope can be larger than the active runway. The initial runway materializes exactly two ordered units. Continuation runways are planned first, while detailed teaching is prepared progressively for only the next unit needed.

## Persistent teaching

Opening a unit creates or returns its persisted teaching content. Concepts and required sections are completed sequentially. The saved lesson position lets Home and Learn resume precisely without regenerating earlier material.

During teaching, the learner can:

- save a note with its source context;
- mark a word or selection and retain its explanation;
- record a doubt or resolve one;
- indicate understanding at supported checkpoints; and
- request deeper clarification.

## Adaptation

Adaptation uses persisted facts: completed concepts and units, lab runs and submissions, independent or assisted evidence, unresolved or resolved doubts, proven knowledge, selected depth, original target, and remaining scope. Self-reported knowledge remains distinct from evidence-backed knowledge.

This produces the next proposed runway. Commerce gates activation, while the learning engine owns the proposal and progressive preparation after verified access.
